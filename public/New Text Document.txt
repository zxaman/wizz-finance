1. Landing, Signup, and Login
01 Base Landing Screen with login and sign up screen
This introduces the product and prompts users to either log in or sign up. It’s always the entry point.

New User Signup Flow:
02 362 - Signup Individual _ Capture Details and Submit
Starts the individual sign up with details entry.

03 446 - Signup - Uniqueness
Confirms that the email/phone is unique and not already registered.

04 368 - Signup individual _ Verify Email & Phone
Verifies provided contact details for account creation.

18 385 - Terms & Condition
Terms and conditions are presented for consent before proceeding.

Social Login and Login Flow:
07 373 - Social Login
Supports sign-in via social accounts.

05 369 - Individual _ Login using email and password
Standard login for returning users.

08 447 - Complete Profile after first social login
Prompts to complete profile on their initial login via social platform.

06 444 - Post Login action - Continue to update profile Information
Directs all new users to finish their profile updates to improve onboarding.

2. Password Recovery
09 378 - Forgot Password Request
Initiates password recovery.

10 379 - Set new Password after reset link
Sets a new password after a reset request.

3. KYC and Identity Verification
11 381 - Upload Identity Document
Uploads ID documents for KYC.

12 445 - Remove Identity Document
Option to remove or re-upload incorrect documents.

13 382 - Complete Liveness Check
Confirms identity via a liveness/selfie test.

4. Beneficiary Management
14 391 - Add Beneficiary Details & Address
Step 1 of adding a new beneficiary (personal/address info).

15 448 - Add Beneficiary - Capture bank details and confirm
Step 2 of adding bank details and confirmation.

26 401 - Select & add beneficiary
Beneficiary is selected or another is added.

16 393 - View Beneficiary List
Review a list of all added beneficiaries.

17 399 - Remove Beneficiary
Option to remove a beneficiary.

5. Payment Methods
19 395 - Add Payment Method - Bank Accounts
Add a bank account as a payment method.

20 397 - Bank Account - Micro Deposit
Verify the bank account by small deposit.

21 449 - Add Payment Method - Card
Add a card as a payment option.

22 450 - Verify Card - Small Charge
Verify the card by a small charge.

27 402 - Select and Add Payment Method
Choose or add a payment method.

23 396 - Remove Payment Methods
Remove a payment method.

6. Transacting
24 400 - Send Money - Initiate Remittance
Initiate a send money/remittance transaction.

7. Compliance & Final Confirmation
25 403 - Compliance, Review & Confirmation
Compliance review (such as last KYC check or confirmation) prior to transaction completion.
